# P2 Healer Assignments — SSC / TK (25)

**Roster (5):**

| Healer | Class | Role bias |
|---|---|---|
| Healparser | Holy Paladin | Tank healing |
| Stormyluna | Resto Shaman | Chain Heal on tanks/melee |
| Colbsterx | Resto Shaman | Chain Heal raid |
| Gipples | Holy Priest | Raid CoH; lock tanks only |
| Tempest | Holy Priest | Raid CoH / flex — never on tanks |

**Standing rules:**
- **Tank comp: Adricus (Prot Paladin, boss tank), Sarixen (Prot Paladin, trash only), Treels (Feral bear).**
- **Tank healers are Healparser, Stormyluna, and Colbsterx — priests are never assigned to tanks. Sole exception: Gipples always heals warlock tanks.**
- **Healparser priority rule:** Healparser heals Adricus wherever Adricus is tanking. Only when Adricus isn't tanking anything does Healparser move (Treels, or per-boss exception below).
- **Shaman rule:** when assigned a tank, cast Chain Heal ON the tank — tank gets the big first heal, the bounces heal nearby melee for free.
- **Earth Shield priority: Adricus > Sarixen > Treels.** One talented shaman = it lives on Adricus. Both talented = second shield follows the same order.
- Priests own magic dispels and PW:S on spike damage.

## Serpentshrine Cavern

### Hydross the Unstable
| Healer | Assignment |
|---|---|
| Healparser | Adricus's form (frost or nature tank slot) |
| Stormyluna | Cast Chain Heal on Treels (opposite form) |
| Colbsterx | Melee/raid Chain Heal (primary target: the active form tank) + transition add tanks |
| Gipples | Raid CoH + Vile Sludge targets |
| Tempest | Raid CoH + Water Tomb targets |

Notes: **Transitions are the highest tank damage window** — 4 adds spawn on the fresh tank each form swap; pre-plan tank CDs and be pre-casting on the incoming tank. Frost form: raid spreads 8yd for Water Tomb (4,500 frost + 4s stun) — heal stunned targets. Nature form: **Vile Sludge cuts healing received by 50%** on the target — Gipples prioritizes them early since heals are half value. Water Tomb only happens in frost form, Sludge only in nature.

| Healer | Assignment |
|---|---|

| Stormyluna | Chain Heal into the melee pack behind Lurker (NOT Treels — he's too far for bounces); on submerge cast on Adricus |

Spout: ranged jump in the water, melee run around the boss — top everyone before it. Whirl knocks melee around — Stormyluna's bounces catch it. Submerge = 9 adds (3 melee platform, 2 per ranged platform, ranged ones CC'd).

Inner Demon (5 random players): keep them topped so they can kill their copy — failure = 10-min unremovable MC. 15% split: both forms active at once, no more Inner Demons — Gipples stays glued to the warlock, everyone else burns heals on the raid.

|---|---|---|

| Stormyluna | Adricus | Treels + raid Chain Heal |
| Colbsterx | Adricus | Raid + Cataclysmic Bolt triage |

After Tidalvess dies, Spitfire Totems pulse raid damage until DPS kill them. Sharkkis's elemental pet explodes on death — heal melee through it.

Flame Quills = everyone drops to the lower ring, expect raid-wide topping after. ## Mana / cooldown notes

- Karathress and Kael are the genuinely tight fights for 5 healers — pre-pot, Dark/Demonic Runes, Shadowfiend + Innervate mapped before pull.
- Shamans stagger Mana Tide — one at ~60% raid mana, one at ~30%.
- Fear Ward assignments (P2 has three fear fights): Solarian P2, Kael Sanguinar, Vashj P2 striders (kiter, if warranted).
- Battle rez priority: healer > tank > dps, always.

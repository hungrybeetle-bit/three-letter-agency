# P2 Healer Assignments — SSC / TK (25)

**Roster (5):**

| Healer | Class | Role bias |
|---|---|---|
| Healparser | Holy Paladin | Tank healing |
| Kumin | Resto Shaman | Chain Heal on tanks/melee |
| Colbsterx | Resto Shaman | Chain Heal raid |
| Gipples | Holy Priest | Raid CoH; lock tanks only |
| Tazianne | Resto Shaman | Raid heals at his own discretion — no fixed boss assignments |

**Standing rules:**
- **Tank comp: Sarixen (Prot Paladin, boss tank), Treels (Feral bear), Hesky (Prot Paladin, trash only).**
- **Tank healers are Healparser, Kumin, and Colbsterx — priests are never assigned to tanks. Sole exception: Gipples always heals warlock tanks.**
- **Healparser priority rule:** Healparser heals Sarixen wherever Sarixen is tanking. Only when Sarixen isn't tanking anything does Healparser move (Treels, or per-boss exception below).
- **Shaman rule:** when assigned a tank, cast Chain Heal ON the tank — tank gets the big first heal, the bounces heal nearby melee for free.
- **Earth Shield priority: Sarixen > Calph > Treels.** One talented shaman = it lives on Sarixen. Both talented = second shield follows the same order.
- Priests own magic dispels and PW:S on spike damage.
- **Tazianne has no fixed assignments** — he raid heals as he sees fit. Exceptions: trash (Chain Heal on Hesky) and Karathress (Caribdis kiter).

## Serpentshrine Cavern

### Hydross the Unstable
| Healer | Assignment |
|---|---|
| Healparser | Sarixen's form (frost or nature tank slot) |
| Kumin | Cast Chain Heal on Treels (opposite form) |
| Colbsterx | Melee/raid Chain Heal (primary target: the active form tank) + transition add tanks |
| Gipples | Raid CoH — Vile Sludge and Water Tomb targets first |

Notes: **Transitions are the highest tank damage window** — 4 adds spawn on the fresh tank each form swap; pre-plan tank CDs and be pre-casting on the incoming tank. Frost form: raid spreads 8yd for Water Tomb (4,500 frost + 4s stun) — heal stunned targets. Nature form: **Vile Sludge cuts healing received by 50%** on the target — Gipples prioritizes them early since heals are half value. Water Tomb only happens in frost form, Sludge only in nature.

Submerge = 9 adds (3 melee platform, 2 per ranged platform, ranged ones CC'd).

Demon form: Chaos Blast ramps on the FR warlock tank — Gipples pre-ramps *before* each swap. Inner Demon (5 random players): Colbsterx keeps them topped so they can kill their copy — failure = 10-min unremovable MC (Gipples is on the lock, he can't). 15% split: both forms active at once, no more Inner Demons — Gipples stays glued to the warlock, everyone else burns heals on the raid.

### Fathom-Lord Karathress
| Healer | Until Tidalvess dies | After |
|---|---|---|
| Healparser | Sarixen | Sarixen |
| Kumin | Sarixen — but watch Treels, don't let him die | Treels + raid Chain Heal |

| Gipples | Raid CoH (fallback: Caribdis kiter if Tazianne dies/is out) | Same |

Notes: Kill order Tidalvess → Sharkkis → Karathress. **Caribdis is never killed — she's kited in the corner all fight.** Tazianne owns her kiter start to finish; if Tazianne dies or is out, Gipples takes over. **Opener: all three tank healers (Healparser, Kumin, Colbsterx) stack heals on Sarixen until Tidalvess (the shaman) is dead**, then Kumin picks up Treels and Colbster goes to raid. During the opener Kumin's on Sarixen with the others but keeps an eye on Treels — throw him a heal if he's dropping, he just can't die. Raid coverage is bounces-only until Tidalvess falls, so keep the burn fast. Tazianne positions with the kite corner (Water Bolt Volley splash). **Cataclysmic Bolt hits random mana users for half their HP — that includes you.** Once free, Colbsterx's whole job is topping Bolt targets instantly. After Tidalvess dies, Spitfire Totems pulse raid damage until DPS kill them. Sharkkis's elemental pet explodes on death — heal melee through it.
